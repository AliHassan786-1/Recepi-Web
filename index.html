Our Popular Recipes

🍝 Pasta

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Boil pasta until tender.
  2. Cook garlic with tomato sauce.
  3. Mix pasta with the sauce and serve.

🍕 Pizza

Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Vegetables of your choice

Instructions:

  1. Spread sauce over the dough.
  2. Add cheese and toppings.
  3. Bake until golden and bubbly.

🥞 Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions:

  1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Pour batter into a hot pan.
  3. Flip, cook until golden, and serve.
